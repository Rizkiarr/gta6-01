# Neo Harbor - Open World Driving Game

Neo Harbor is a sun-soaked modern harbor city where you explore on foot and by car. Steal vehicles, complete missions, and evade the police in this original open-world crime game.

## The City

Neo Harbor features 12 distinct districts including Downtown, Harbor, West Beach, Oakwood, East Harbor, Docks, Airport, Hills, Suburbs, Industrial, Sunset Park, and Riverside. The city has a network of roads, highways, parks, beaches, and dense urban areas to explore.

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Walk/Run
- **E**: Enter/Exit vehicles
- **Space**: Handbrake (while driving) / Melee attack (on foot)
- **ESC**: Pause menu

## Gameplay Features

### Vehicles
- Drive sports cars, sedans, SUVs, and motorcycles
- Realistic acceleration, braking, and steering
- Camera smoothly follows your vehicle

### Traffic System
- AI-driven traffic that follows lanes and avoids collisions
- Pedestrians walking sidewalks and crossing streets

### Wanted Level (0-5 stars)
- Increases when you steal cars, speed, or hit people
- Decreases when you stay out of sight
- Police chase you with patrol cars and foot officers

### Missions
1. **Easy Drift** - Drift for 10 seconds in the parking lot
2. **Harbor Speed Run** - Reach 120 mph on Coastal Highway
3. **Cargo Heist** - Steal and deliver a cargo truck
4. **Federal Investigation** - Escape with max wanted level for 60 seconds

### Combat
- Melee attacks on foot (Space)
- Vehicle horns as ranged attacks
- Realistic damage system with health tracking

### HUD
- Health, Money, Wanted stars, Speed displays
- Live minimap showing player, vehicles, police, and mission markers
- Current mission objective

## How to Run

1. Serve the `game/` directory with any static HTTP server
2. Open `index.html` in a desktop browser (Chrome/Edge/Firefox)
3. Click "START GAME" or use keyboard controls directly

## Development

Built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and WebAudio. No external libraries or network requests. All assets are procedurally generated.
